Theory into flight hardware

I'm an aerospace engineering student at Amity University Dubai and a propulsion researcher. My work runs from plasma physics and particle-in-cell simulation to motor casings, test stands and vacuum chambers.

I've worked on electric thrusters at ISRO's Liquid Propulsion Systems Centre, Airbus aerostructures at EDGE, and a VLEO mission concept for the European Space Agency. At university I founded ASTREX, the UAE's first university-level rocketry team, and I manage Amity GLEE, the UAE's first ChipSat, built for possible flight on NASA Artemis lunar missions.
